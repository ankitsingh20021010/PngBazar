Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PNG Bazar handles information when you use our website.

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PNG Bazar. We provide free transparent PNG images and related resources for personal, educational, creative and other lawful uses.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit PNG Bazar, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

By using PNG Bazar, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

PNG Bazar is primarily a publicly accessible website. We do not require users to create an account to browse or download PNG images.

Depending on how you use the website, certain information may be collected automatically by our website or by third-party services.

Information that may be collected automatically

  • IP address or approximate location
  • Browser and device information
  • Operating system information
  • Pages visited and referring pages
  • Date and time of website visits
  • General website usage information

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining PNG Bazar
  • Improving website performance and usability
  • Understanding website traffic and usage
  • Detecting technical problems or abuse
  • Improving our content and services
  • Displaying advertisements where applicable

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

PNG Bazar and certain third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies are small data files that can be stored on your device when you visit a website. They may help websites remember preferences, understand traffic and provide relevant functionality.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Advertising

PNG Bazar may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including Google and its advertising services.

These advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to understand interactions with advertisements and to provide advertising services.

Third-party advertising providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the applicable policies of those providers for additional information.

6. Google Advertising Services

If Google advertising services are used on PNG Bazar, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings and policies.

Google may use information collected through advertising technologies in accordance with its applicable privacy policies.

Users may have options to manage advertising personalization through Google's available advertising settings and controls.

7. Analytics and Website Performance

We may use analytics or similar technologies to understand how visitors use PNG Bazar.

Analytics information can help us understand page views, traffic sources, device types, popular content and general website performance.

8. Third-Party Websites

PNG Bazar may contain links to third-party websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or policies of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies before providing personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

PNG Bazar is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information associated with our website. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of PNG Bazar, you can contact us.
