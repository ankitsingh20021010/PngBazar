DMCA Policy

Learn how to report copyright concerns and request the removal of content from PNG Bazar.

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

1. Copyright Policy

PNG Bazar respects the intellectual property rights of copyright owners and expects users of the website to do the same.

If you believe that material available on PNG Bazar infringes your copyright, you may submit a copyright infringement notice using the process described below.

2. How to Submit a Copyright Notice

A copyright owner or an authorized representative may contact PNG Bazar with a written notice containing sufficient information for us to identify the allegedly infringing material.

To help us process a request, please include:

  1. Your full name and contact information.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work that you believe has been infringed.
  3. The URL or specific location of the material on PNG Bazar.
  4. A statement explaining why you believe the material infringes your copyright.
  5. A statement confirming that the information in your notice is accurate.
  6. A statement that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
  7. Your electronic or physical signature.

3. Where to Send a DMCA Request

Please send copyright-related requests to the contact address below.

4. Review and Removal

After receiving a sufficiently detailed copyright complaint, PNG Bazar may review the reported material and take appropriate action where necessary.

Depending on the circumstances, action may include restricting access to or removing the reported material.

Submission of a notice does not automatically mean that content will be removed. Requests may require additional information or clarification.

5. Counter-Notification

If content was removed or restricted because of a copyright complaint and you believe the removal was made in error, you may contact PNG Bazar with an explanation and relevant information supporting your position.

A counter-notification should clearly identify the affected content and explain why you believe the material should be restored.

Any counter-notification may be reviewed before further action is taken.

6. Repeat Infringement

PNG Bazar may take appropriate action against accounts, submissions or activities that are repeatedly associated with copyright infringement, where applicable.

7. Good-Faith Complaints

Copyright complaints should be submitted in good faith and should contain accurate information.

Please do not submit false or misleading claims about copyright ownership or infringement.
